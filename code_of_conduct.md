# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep the gallery technical. Personal attacks, harassment, and off-topic political content will be removed and the contributor warned or banned at the maintainers' discretion.

Report incidents to conduct@agentr.dev.
